# Product Principles

## 1. Reduce mental load

NIDO must remove planning work, not move it into a phone.

If the caregiver has to continually maintain the system, the system has failed.

## 2. Show one decision at a time

The Today screen emphasizes **Now**.

Do not present the whole backlog when the user is holding a child and needs the next action.

## 3. Reality wins

The routine is a plan.

Actual events become new inputs.

The system adapts instead of punishing variance.

## 4. Be deterministic where safety/trust matter

Scheduling rules, care-plan guardrails and conflict resolution are deterministic.

AI can explain; it is not the authority.

## 5. Explain every meaningful change

If a nap moves, NIDO can say why.

No black-box “AI optimization.”

## 6. The caregiver is authoritative

Manual override is always possible.

Do not fight the user by automatically undoing explicit choices.

## 7. Attention is scarce

A notification must earn the interruption.

Not everything on the routine deserves a notification.

## 8. Difficult days are still valid days

No streaks.
No failure badges.
No guilt.

Chaos Mode should simplify rather than shame.

## 9. Track only what changes decisions

Do not create a second job called “maintaining NIDO.”

## 10. Protect professional instructions

Clinician/dietitian/therapist instructions can be represented as locked Care Plan constraints.

## 11. Work offline

The caregiver can be in a park, basement or airplane mode and still operate the day.

## 12. System surfaces are part of the product

Watch, Lock Screen, widgets and Siri are not marketing add-ons. They reduce the need to open the app.
