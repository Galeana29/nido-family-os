# Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

NIDO uses four primary spaces:

```text
Today
Plan
Insights
Profile
```

Quick Log is globally available and is not a fifth information architecture destination.

## Today

Mental question:

> What matters now?

Contains:

- current action;
- next events;
- active program;
- personal caregiver window;
- exceptions.

Does not contain:

- deep analytics;
- long settings;
- full weekly editing.

## Plan

Mental question:

> What is the shape of the day/week?

Subsections:

```text
Day
Meals
Programs
Household
```

### Day
Resolved timeline.

### Meals
Meal plan, food guidance, substitutions and shopping link.

### Programs
Gradual routine programs.

### Household
Preparation and grocery tasks.

## Insights

Mental question:

> What patterns are emerging?

Natural-language interpretation first.
Data second.

## Profile

Contains:

- household;
- people;
- permissions;
- notification preferences;
- care plan sources;
- integrations;
- privacy/export;
- developer/debug info where enabled.

## People are not navigation silos

A user should not need to choose “Mom” or “Child” before acting.

Owner appears contextually.

Today composes the household timeline.
