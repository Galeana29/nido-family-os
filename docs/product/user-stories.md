# User Stories

## Core caregiver

### Now

As a caregiver, I want to open NIDO and immediately know what matters now so I do not have to mentally scan the day.

### Replanning

As a caregiver, I want the rest of the day to adjust after a late nap so I do not have to manually recalculate every event.

### Explainability

As a caregiver, I want to know why an event moved so I can trust the adjustment.

### Control

As a caregiver, I want to override NIDO when real life requires it and have NIDO respect the decision.

### Meal

As a caregiver, I want to record that a child ate “a little” without measuring grams so the log is useful but sustainable.

### Refusal

As a caregiver, I want immediate contextual guidance when a meal is rejected instead of searching through documentation.

### Nap

As a caregiver, I want to tap “asleep” and “awake” and let NIDO handle the downstream schedule.

### One hand

As a caregiver holding a child, I want the important actions available in one or two large taps.

### Voice

As a caregiver with both hands occupied, I want to say “she woke up ten minutes ago” and have NIDO prepare the correct event.

### Watch

As a caregiver without easy access to my phone, I want to log the end of a nap from my wrist.

### Calendar

As a caregiver, I want NIDO to notice a doctor appointment and reorganize flexible parts of the routine around it.

### Difficult day

As a caregiver on a chaotic day, I want NIDO to show only what is essential rather than display a wall of incomplete tasks.

### Personal time

As a caregiver, when my child sleeps I want NIDO to protect that window instead of automatically filling it with chores.

### Care Plan

As a parent following professional advice, I want that instruction preserved in the system so automation cannot silently contradict it.

## Second caregiver

As a second caregiver, I want to see that a meal or nap was already logged so I do not duplicate care.

## Pediatric visit

As a parent, I want to summarize a selected period of observations for a clinician without presenting NIDO as a diagnostic authority.
